ExcedentMarket is a B2B marketplace for industrial surplus and waste materials in Paraguay. It connects **generators** — companies with industrial by-products — with **recyclers** who valorize those materials, all within the regulatory framework of Ley N.° 3956/2009. The platform handles the full transactional cycle: catalog browsing, offer negotiation, contract generation, payment processing, and environmental impact reporting.

## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Register your company">
    Generators and recyclers create accounts at `/register`. The platform validates the company's RUC against the Paraguay DNIT taxpayer registry and checks environmental licenses against the MADES SIAM system.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish or browse lots">
    Generators publish waste lots — specifying material, quantity, price, and location — through their Filament panel. Recyclers browse the public marketplace at `/marketplace` and filter by material category, department, or city.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Negotiate and contract">
    Recyclers submit offers; generators can accept, reject, or counter-offer. Once accepted, the system automatically generates a PDF contract and a digitally verifiable transmission certificate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay and close">
    The buyer pays through Pagopar or DLocalGo. On confirmation, inventory is updated, CO₂ savings are recorded, and the lot closes. All documents carry a QR code verifiable at `/verificar/{codigo}`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Key Capabilities

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Geographic Filtering" icon="map-location-dot">
    Filter lots by Paraguay department and city. A 5 km proximity search surfaces nearby lots on each lot detail page.
  </Card>
  <Card title="CO₂ Tracking" icon="leaf">
    Every completed transaction records estimated CO₂ savings based on material emission factors, feeding the admin environmental dashboard.
  </Card>
  <Card title="ODS Metrics" icon="chart-line">
    The platform tracks Sustainable Development Goal indicators (SDG 12, 13) via scheduled jobs that update `metricas_ods` automatically.
  </Card>
  <Card title="ARCO Privacy Rights" icon="shield-halved">
    Full compliance with Ley N.° 7.593/2025: users can request data exports, rectifications, cancellations, and objections from their privacy panel.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Document Verification" icon="qrcode">
    Contracts, manifests, and certification documents include QR codes linking to `/verificar/{codigo}` — accessible to regulators without an account.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Audit Trail" icon="clock-rotate-left">
    Every state-changing operation is logged to `audit_logs` with actor, action, and timestamp for full regulatory traceability.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
